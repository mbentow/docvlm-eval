# Corpus format, and an honest note about the bundled one

← [back to README](../README.md)

---

## The format is deliberately boring

A clever format is why nobody uses your evaluator — including you, six months from now.

```
corpora/my-corpus/
├─ manifest.jsonl
├─ schema.py
└─ images/
```

One JSON object per line:

```jsonl
{"id":"0001","image":"images/0001.jpg","truth":{"patient_name":"Marina Ferrazzo","doctor_crm":"20154-RS","insurer":null,"exams":["Holter 24h"],"urgent":false},"tags":["handwritten","phone_photo","low_light"]}
```

`schema.py` declares the fields and how each one is compared — see
[Declaring how a field is compared](../README.md#declaring-how-a-field-is-compared).

### Validate before you spend GPU hours

```bash
docvlm-eval validate --corpus corpora/my-corpus
```

It checks missing images, duplicate ids, and — the one that actually bites — **ground-truth
keys that are not in the schema**. Without that check, a typo in one key shows up as a field
permanently stuck at 0% while you go looking for the cause in the model.

### Tag generously

Tags are the difference between "78% accuracy" and "36% under motion blur". They cost nothing
at annotation time and they are the only thing in the report that tells you what to build
next. Tag for input condition (`handwritten`, `phone_photo`, `low_light`, `glare`, `skewed`,
`motion_blur`), not for outcome.

And read the `n` column. A tag with two documents is a hint, not a finding.

## The bundled synthetic corpus

`corpora/synthetic-forms` and `corpora/synthetic-forms-hard` are generated by
`tools/generate_synthetic_corpus.py` from a fixed seed. Every value is fabricated. They exist
so anyone can run this tool without having documents of their own.

```bash
python tools/generate_synthetic_corpus.py \
  --out corpora/synthetic-forms-hard -n 60 --hard --seed 424242
```

Run that and you get the committed corpus back, byte for byte.

> [!NOTE]
> Patients, doctors, clinics and insurers are all invented — the insurer names are deliberately
> *not* real trade names, because a recognisable brand on a fake medical form would make "every
> value is fabricated" true only with an asterisk. Every image carries a visible watermark:
> *SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — Not a real request.*

### The clean version saturates, and that is worth reporting

| corpus | model | macro | all-fields-correct | hallucination |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `synthetic-forms` | qwen3-vl:8b | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.00% |
| `synthetic-forms` | qwen3-vl:30b-a3b | 0.981 | 0.867 | 0.24% |
| `synthetic-forms-hard` | qwen3-vl:8b | 0.781 | 0.583 | 0.00% |
| `synthetic-forms-hard` | qwen3-vl:30b-a3b | 0.752 | 0.533 | 0.48% |

**A benchmark everybody passes measures nothing.** Rendered fonts — even jittered and slanted —
are far easier than photographed handwriting. `--hard` reproduces what actually breaks
extraction in the field: low effective resolution, motion blur, glare, a stamp across the
licence number. That is what produced the numbers at the top of the README.

### Treat it as a smoke test, not as evidence

The synthetic corpus tells you the pipeline works and shows you the shape of a report. It tells
you **nothing** about your documents.

The corpus is the part that matters, and it is the part only you can build. Sixty of your own
documents with honest ground truth are worth more than six thousand of anyone else's.
